Built for keepers, not spreadsheets

A timeline per animal

Feedings with the outcome, sheds with the phase, weights, lengths, vet visits and notes. Seven built-in entry types are free always; if one is missing, add your own.

Reminders that go quiet on their own

A feeding schedule falls silent while the animal is in shed or brumation, and says why. One overdue item stays one item, however long you were away.

Growth you can trust

Weight and length on one chart, drawn from what you actually wrote down. Lengths measured in Snake Measurer arrive on their own and stay read-only.

What stays free, in writing

Put down now, while the diary is new, so that it cannot quietly move once you have a year of records in it.

  • Free: 3 animals, 3 places, every entry and every state, 1 schedule, 1 entry type of your own, 1 helper per animal.
  • Reading, editing, deleting and exporting your own records is free forever, whatever the plan.
  • Anything paid is paid from the day it appears. Nothing is given away and fenced off later.
  • People you invite never pay — the owner of the animal does.
  • No trial period: a two-week clock cannot show a reminder that fires every ten days.

Before you start

Do I need an account?
No. Open the diary and add an animal — an unnamed account is created for you and everything is saved on the server. Link Google, Apple, email or Telegram later to keep access from other devices: the key to an unnamed account lives only in this browser.
How will a feeding reminder reach me?
Today it does not: tasks appear when you open the diary. Delivery of reminders is the next thing being built.
What does it cost?
Free: 3 animals, 3 places, every entry and state, 1 schedule, 1 entry type of your own, 1 helper per animal. Reading, editing and exporting your data is free always. More comes with Premium, which is bought in Snake Measurer.
Where do my photos go?
Photos are stored with us, in private storage tied to your account, and are never passed to third parties.
Can someone else log for my animals?
Yes. Invite a helper by email or link; they can log entries, close tasks and run their own schedules on your animal. Helpers never pay.
